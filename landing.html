How we score you…

Your session grade combines posture alignment with the amount of time spent in an uncomfortable position.

What we measure

Head and neck position Forward head movement is measured using the craniovertebral angle.
Spine position Forward trunk or spine flexion is compared with your calibrated baseline.
Shoulder alignment Differences between your left and right shoulders are measured.
Pelvis alignment Differences in hip or pelvis tilt are measured.

How your score is calculated

Your score starts at 100. Points are deducted when your posture moves beyond the established alignment thresholds. Larger deviations result in larger penalties [1].

The score also considers how long you remain in poor posture. A rolling average helps prevent the score from changing abruptly [1].

Score ranges

90–100 Strong posture
70–89 Could be better
50–69 Time to refocus
0–49 Consider taking a break and stretching

The posture score uses calibrated anatomical deviations, time-based exposure, and rolling averaging [1]. It is an ergonomic estimate, not a medical assessment.
